OV²SLAM is a fully online visual SLAM system that runs in real time on monocular and stereo cameras. It combines fast front-end tracking with continuous local bundle adjustment and an online bag-of-words for loop closure, so it needs no pre-trained vocabulary.

- Published in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters and presented at ICRA 2021 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.04060))
- Ranked 3rd in the CVPR 2020 TartanAir visual SLAM challenge
- Open source: [github.com/ov2slam/ov2slam](https://github.com/ov2slam/ov2slam)
