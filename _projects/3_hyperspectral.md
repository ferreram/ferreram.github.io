description: Combining hyperspectral imaging and 3D reconstruction for underwater surveys

This work fuses hyperspectral push-broom imagery with visual 3D reconstruction to produce georeferenced hyperspectral 3D maps of the seafloor, opening the way to finer habitat and substrate analysis during underwater surveys.

- Presented at the ICCV 2021 Computer Vision in the Ocean workshop ([paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021W/OceanVision/papers/Ferrera_Hyperspectral_3D_Mapping_of_Underwater_Environments_ICCVW_2021_paper.pdf))
