AQUALOC is a dataset recorded with a monocular camera, an IMU and a pressure sensor mounted on remotely operated vehicles, at depths from a few meters to several hundred meters. It was built to benchmark localization and SLAM methods in real underwater conditions: turbidity, poor lighting, moving fish and sediment.

- Published in the International Journal of Robotics Research, 2019 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.14532))
- Dataset and tools: [lirmm.fr/aqualoc](http://www.lirmm.fr/aqualoc/)
