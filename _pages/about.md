I am a research scientist in the Underwater Robotics Lab at [Ifremer](https://www.ifremer.fr/), the French Research Institute for Exploitation of the Sea, in La Seyne-sur-Mer. I work on robot perception and computer vision for underwater vehicles.

Before joining Ifremer, I was a postdoctoral researcher at [ONERA](https://www.onera.fr/), the French Aerospace Lab, working on visual SLAM. I did my PhD at ONERA and [LIRMM](https://www.lirmm.fr/) on vision-based localization and 3D mapping for underwater robots.

My research interests are visual SLAM, 3D reconstruction, sensor fusion and machine learning, with a focus on making them work in the turbid, dynamic and poorly lit conditions found underwater.

**I am looking for students.** I regularly supervise PhD students and interns. Email me if you would like to work with me.
