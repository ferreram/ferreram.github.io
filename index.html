Maxime Ferrera

Research Scientist, Ifremer, Underwater Robotics Lab

Ifremer, Underwater Robotics Lab

La Seyne-sur-Mer, France

I am a research scientist in the Underwater Robotics Lab at Ifremer, the French Research Institute for Exploitation of the Sea, in La Seyne-sur-Mer. I work on robot perception and computer vision for underwater vehicles.

Before joining Ifremer, I was a postdoctoral researcher at ONERA, the French Aerospace Lab, working on visual SLAM. I did my PhD at ONERA and LIRMM on vision-based localization and 3D mapping for underwater robots.

My research interests are visual SLAM, 3D reconstruction, sensor fusion and machine learning, with a focus on making them work in the turbid, dynamic and poorly lit conditions found underwater.

I am looking for students. I regularly supervise PhD students and interns. Email me if you would like to work with me.
